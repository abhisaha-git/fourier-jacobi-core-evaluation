# Supporting proof and review reports

These reports retain the detailed row maps, monomial calculations, actual
measure proofs and independent reviews produced during the development.
The current strongest statement is in `../statement.md`; its full human proof
is in `../proof.md`. The final source-matched build and exhaustive audit are
recorded in `../../VERIFICATION.md`.

The I0, I1 and I2 reports supply the fifty-row proof details alongside
`../source-map.md`. The source-restoration audit distinguishes raw printed
fractions from their already restored stored fractions. It is supplementary
symbolic evidence, not a proof certificate.

The integer-shell, multiplicative-Haar, collision, kernel, and cell-value/mass
reports explain the actual field infrastructure. The Stage A and Haar reviews
check that definitions and theorem hypotheses do not conceal the desired
bridge. Their developmental check logs or recorded module hashes are historical
snapshots; the final verification record covers the entire delivered sources,
including subsequent assembly and the elementary scalar-denominator lemma.

Some workspace commands in these reports refer to preserved source material
outside this standalone publication. Ordinary proof reproduction requires only
the sources and pinned manifest delivered here. The raw TeX snippets and exact
row data needed for a standalone supplementary restoration comparison are
included under `../../data/`.
