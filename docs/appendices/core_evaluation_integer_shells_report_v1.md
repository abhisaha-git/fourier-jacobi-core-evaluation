# Actual local-field integer shell volumes, version 1

The new module is
`publications/fourier-jacobi-core-evaluation/FourierJacobi/Measure/IntegerShells.lean`.
It supplies a Stage B ingredient. It does not identify a kernel integral with
the master series or a spherical coefficient with the explicit Weyl kernel.

Let `K` be any field with a valuative relation, topology, and mathlib's
`IsNonarchimedeanLocalField K` instance. Give it its Borel measurable space.
Let `q=residueCardinality K` be the cardinality of its actual residue field,
let `μ=additiveHaar K` be the Haar measure independently constructed and
normalized by `μ(𝒪[K])=1` in the inherited `LocalHaar` module, and let
`v=localFieldValuation K : K → WithTop ℤ` be the normalized additive
valuation independently constructed in the inherited `LocalField` module.
In particular `v(0)=∞`.

For every integer `n`, define

\[
B_n=\{x\in K:v(x)\ge n\},\qquad
S_n=\{x\in K:v(x)=n\}.
\]

The new declarations in namespace `FourierJacobi.Measure` prove:

- `canonical_uniformizer_generates`: any integral element with canonical
  additive valuation one generates the actual maximal ideal. This conclusion
  is proved rather than supplied as an extra uniformizer hypothesis.
- `canonical_uniformizer_zpow`: `v(π^n)=n` for every integer `n`, including
  negative powers.
- `integerBall_eq_uniformizer_smul`: `B_n=π^n𝒪[K]` as actual subsets of `K`.
- `integerBall_isOpen`, `integerBall_measurableSet`, and
  `integerShell_measurableSet`: these are measurable actual valuation loci.
- `integerShell_pairwiseDisjoint`, `existsUnique_integerShell`, and
  `iUnion_integerShell_eq`: the shells form a countable disjoint partition of
  `K\{0}` with both uniqueness and exhaustion proved.
- `canonicalUniformizer_distribHaarChar`: the actual Haar scaling character
  of `π` is `q⁻¹`.
- `additiveHaar_integerBall`, `additiveHaar_integerBall_ne_top`, and
  `additiveHaar_real_integerBall`: every `B_n` has finite volume `q^(-n)`.
- `additiveHaar_real_integerShell`: every integer shell has volume

  \[
  \mu(S_n)=(1-q^{-1})q^{-n}.
  \]

  The same statement in the measure's native extended nonnegative-real
  codomain is `additiveHaar_integerShell`. The shell volume is finite and
  strictly positive by `additiveHaar_integerShell_ne_top` and
  `additiveHaar_real_integerShell_pos`.

- `canonicalValuation_measurable`: the complete extended valuation map is
  measurable, including its infinite value at zero. Since all fibers are
  measurable and the codomain is countable, this holds for any chosen
  measurable structure on `WithTop ℤ`, in particular its Borel structure.

The real-measure declarations use `Measure.real`, the real value of the
actual Haar measure. Finiteness of the relevant balls is proved first, so
these are not interpretations of an infinite measure as the `toReal`
convention. The inherited theorem `residueCardinality_one_lt` proves `q>1`
from the actual residue field; no zero denominator is hidden here. No
characteristic-zero, oddness, Satake-regularity, or residue-primality
restriction is needed for this geometric result.

## Mathematical proof

The canonical multiplicative valuation has value group `ℤᵐ⁰`, and the
extended additive valuation sends `exp(a)` to `-a` and sends zero to infinity.
Thus `v(x)=n` is equivalent to the canonical multiplicative value
`exp(-n)`. The discreteness of the integers gives

\[
z<1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad z\le\exp(-1)
\]

for every element `z` of that multiplicative value group, including zero.
Surjectivity of the canonical valuation supplies an element `π` with `v(π)=1`.
It is integral and nonzero. The maximal ideal consists of integral elements
of multiplicative value less than one; the principal ideal generated by `π`
consists of integral elements of value at most the value of `π`. The preceding
discrete comparison proves equality of these two ideals.

The multiplicative valuation maps every integer power `π^n` to `exp(-n)`.
Consequently multiplication by `π^n` adds `n` to the extended additive
valuation and gives `B_n=π^n𝒪[K]`. Multiplication by a nonzero field element
is a homeomorphism, so every such ball is open and measurable. The shell
identity `S_n=B_n\B_{n+1}` is proved in `WithTop ℤ`; its proof keeps the
infinite valuation at zero and excludes zero from every finite shell.

The inherited `LocalHaar` theorem gives the maximal ideal volume `q⁻¹` by
the finite additive index of the actual ideal. Mathlib's `distribHaarChar`
is the independently constructed positive Haar scaling character of the
group `Kˣ` acting on the additive group of `K`. It satisfies

\[
\mu(aE)=\Delta(a)\mu(E),\qquad
\Delta(ab)=\Delta(a)\Delta(b).
\]

Apply this identity to `E=𝒪[K]` and the just-proved generator `π`.
Since `μ(E)=1` and `μ(πE)=q⁻¹`, it follows that `Δ(π)=q⁻¹`.
The group homomorphism law then gives `Δ(π^n)=q^(-n)` for every integer `n`.
This proves the volume formula for all `B_n`, including negative depths.
It also proves their finiteness. Subtract the two nested measurable ball
volumes to obtain

\[
\mu(S_n)=q^{-n}-q^{-(n+1)}=(1-q^{-1})q^{-n}.
\]

Finally every finite valuation fiber is a measurable shell, and the infinite
fiber is exactly `{0}`, a measurable singleton. The codomain `WithTop ℤ` is
countable, so these fiber statements prove measurability of the full
canonical valuation map.

No Haar scaling law, shell volume formula, integrability assertion, or
integral-to-series equality is a field of a newly postulated structure. The
only nontrivial measure results used here are existing mathlib Haar theory
and the previously verified actual maximal-ideal volumes.
